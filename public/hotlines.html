988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
🟢 Available 24/7/365 • Free & Confidential

The national mental health emergency number for the United States and Canada. Provides immediate support for anyone experiencing thoughts of suicide, severe anxiety, substance use challenges, or acute emotional distress.
