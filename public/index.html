Our Safety & Privacy Guarantee to Students

Students often avoid campus counseling or commercial apps out of fear that their struggles will follow their academic records. MindHaven collects zero personal identifying information. Your identity is a randomly generated pseudonym, chat logs are wiped from memory, and all mood logs belong entirely to your anonymous token.

Crisis Warning: If you are experiencing acute thoughts of suicide, self-harm, or severe psychiatric distress, please dial 988 (US & Canada) or contact your campus police/health center immediately. MindHaven is a student-focused hackathon demo engineered for empathetic peer connection.
